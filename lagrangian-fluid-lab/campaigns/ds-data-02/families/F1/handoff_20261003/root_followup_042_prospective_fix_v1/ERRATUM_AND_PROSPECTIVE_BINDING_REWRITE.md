## 1. Minimal Erratum for Commit 96710d4d (Owner 041)

In commit `96710d4d`, the fallback XML physical mother height changes ($H_0 = 0.15\,\text{m}$ for the eccentric obstacle configuration and $H_0 = 0.30\,\text{m}$ for the asymmetric dual-channel configuration) are acknowledged as physically plausible geometric adjustments. However, the scientific reports and outcome assertions presented in `root_followup_041_bounded_fallback_v1` are **formally rejected as proof**.

All bytes of `root_followup_041_bounded_fallback_v1` remain preserved without modification. The following specific claims in Owner 041 are retracted and repudiated:

1. **Unproven 75% Peak Kinetic Energy Suppression**:
   - *Owner 041 Claim*: Reducing initial fluid height $H_0$ from $0.30\,\text{m}$ to $0.15\,\text{m}$ was asserted to cut peak kinetic energy by $75\%$ ($E_k \sim M g H_0 \propto H_0^2$).
   - *Correction*: While initial static potential energy scales quadratically with depth, in unsteady 3D dam-break flows with complex wave impact and 3D asymmetric diversion, peak kinetic energy cannot be mathematically deduced or proven from initial potential energy scaling alone. Peak kinetic suppression is an unverified hypothesis, not an established fact.

2. **Unproven Laminar Stable Stagnation**:
   - *Owner 041 Claim*: Lower forcing was asserted to produce a "laminar and structurally stable" stagnation line on the obstacle and divider.
   - *Correction*: In high-Reynolds free-surface transient flows, reducing initial depth does not eliminate boundary-layer turbulence, stagnation-point instability, or vortex shedding. Stagnation-line stability cannot be claimed without native solver evidence.

3. **Invented Guarantees on Fate Switches, Overtopping, and Droplet Loss ($N_{\text{out}} = 0$)**:
   - *Owner 041 Claim*: Asserted that zero fluid overtops the obstacle ($100\%$ channeled laterally), zero fate switches occur across perturbations, and downstream droplet loss ($N_{\text{out}}$) is eliminated.
   - *Correction*: In Lagrangian SPH simulations, particle trajectory bifurcation near dividing edges remains sensitive to spatial and temporal discretization. Fate stability, overtopping bounds, and splash containment are prospective hypotheses that must be measured from actual simulation outputs, never guaranteed a priori.

4. **Retraction of Unauthorized Gate ($|v_y|_{\text{max}} > 0.4\,\text{m/s}$)**:
   - *Owner 041 Claim*: Introduced an acceptance requirement that simulations must demonstrate $|v_y|_{\text{max}} > 0.4\,\text{m/s}$ to be accepted.
   - *Correction*: This gate was **invented without Root authorization** and is hereby **completely retracted**. No arbitrary velocity cutoff or pass/fail gate is imposed.

- For $dp = 0.005\,\text{m}$, the theoretical continuous mass is $1000 \times (0.005)^3 = 0.000125\,\text{kg}$. In single-precision float32, this is represented as `0.0001250000059371814 kg` (as observed in Root 026 metadata).
- Demanding decimal string equality ($|\Delta M| / M_0 = 0.0$ down to machine zero) is physically and numerically inappropriate.
- Actual initial typed QA must report the **native floating-point weights** generated by GenCase and used by the solver, preserving the solver's exact numeric representations.
